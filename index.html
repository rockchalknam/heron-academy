Template Levels: Import & Export

Import a template

  1. Click Download template on the exercise card. It goes to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open the game with Open Heron Rush, then open the Editor.
  3. Click the Import Level tab at the top.
  4. Pick the file from Downloads and click Open.
  5. The level shows up in your list with "Import" at the end of its name. Click the 🛠 tools button to edit it.

Export your level (backup + turn-in)

  1. In the Editor's level list, tick the checkbox next to one level. (Export only works with one level ticked.)
  2. Click the Export Level tab at the top. A file downloads.
  3. Rename it to your name and level, like NamN-PortalPass.txt.
  4. Upload it in the turn-in form, or save it to your Google Drive as a backup.

Export every Friday. If your laptop gets reset, you can import your levels right back.

How to Turn In

1. Take your proof

  • Screenshot: Win + Shift + S, then drag a box around the game. (PrtSc works too.)
  • No shortcut working? Open the Snipping Tool app from the Start menu and click the camera button.
  • Screenshots save to Pictures → Screenshots.
  • Every exercise is turned in with screenshots. No videos needed.

2. Send it in

  1. Click Turn In on the exercise card (or the button below).
  2. Pick the exercise name from the list.
  3. Upload your screenshot (or exported level file, if the exercise asks for one).
  4. Answer any questions the exercise asks.
